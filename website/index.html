Test marks are not outcomes.

Different test thresholds and reported fatality rates prompt a question. They do not prove one caused the other.

THE DISTINCTION

Licence testing can assess test performance. Safer roads also require people to follow rules consistently in real conditions—and a system that responds when they do not.

02 / THE ENFORCEMENT GAP

A rule only works
when the chain holds.

A violation must be detected, a penalty must follow, and compliance must be tracked. Weak links can make even strict rules feel optional.

What happens after the fine?

Unpaid penalties and repeat violations raise questions about collection, fair process and whether consequences are applied consistently. The article summary points to weak enforcement as part of the problem.
